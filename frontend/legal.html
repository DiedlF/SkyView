Data sources, attribution, and safety notes

Skyview visualizes numerical weather model data for situational awareness. It is not an official weather service and is not certified aviation weather.

DWD attribution

Skyview uses meteorological forecast data from Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) Open Data. The data is processed, cached, transformed, derived, and visualized by Skyview.

Source: Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) Open Data, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). DWD legal notices are available at dwd.de.

DWD is not responsible for Skyview, its processing logic, its derived products, or its visualizations.

Weather and aviation disclaimer

Skyview is an informational forecast visualization tool. It must not be used as the sole basis for operational flight planning, go/no-go decisions, or safety-critical decisions.

Forecasts may be incomplete, delayed, unavailable, stale, or wrong. Always use official briefing sources, official aviation weather products where required, current observations, airspace information, NOTAMs, and pilot judgment.

Processed data

Skyview may display or derive products from ICON-D2 and ICON-EU forecast fields, including convection, wind, clouds, precipitation, significant-weather symbols, diagnostics, point values, meteograms, emagrams, and nowcast-style summaries.

Derived values are computed by Skyview and can differ from official DWD products. Visual color scales, symbols, and thresholds are application-specific interpretations.

Service availability

Skyview depends on upstream data availability, successful ingest runs, cache state, hosting, and network connectivity. No availability, timeliness, or completeness guarantee is provided.

The map uses third-party basemap tiles and frontend libraries. Their own licenses and service terms may apply. See the Privacy Policy for current third-party network requests.
